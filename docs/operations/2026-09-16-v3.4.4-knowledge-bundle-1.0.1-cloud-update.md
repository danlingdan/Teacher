## 变更内容

- `/opt/sqlteacher/shared/official-db-concepts-1.0.1.zip`（21,084,531 bytes，SHA-256
  `f738f729afcd14f41b3921d4bf6b0f025c4873c8cb9d226f15d99acbeaf5fbb3`）新增并经 env 切换生效；
